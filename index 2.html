How to check whether the encoder works

First generate the three-step test. The background should repeat at the selected period, while the raised steps use slightly shorter spacing. If the guide dots fuse but depth does not emerge, try 8–16 px strength and a larger physical display. Avoid viewing a portrait preview scaled down to phone width.
