Atlas Parallax Studio V2

Upload a depth map or photo, generate a genuine single-image parallel-eye stereogram, and export it as a PNG. Runs entirely in your browser, including on GitHub Pages.

1. Source image

Depth map: white = near, black = far. A regular photo has no reliable depth information; brightness mode is experimental.

2. Optical controls

Ready. Start with Quick cube test.

3. Stereogram preview

Parallel-eye: look through the image until the two dots become three. The central dot indicates fusion. View the PNG at a comfortable physical size; scaling changes the effective repeat spacing.

Depth map preview

This encoder uses row-wise binocular correspondence constraints and foreground occlusion checks rather than simply shifting a noisy texture. It is an experimental stereogram tool: real-world fusion depends on display size, eye spacing, vision, and viewing distance. Browser exports are previews, not print-ready 43 × 80-inch masters.
