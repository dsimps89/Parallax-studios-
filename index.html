Atlas Parallax Studio

GitHub Pages edition · Make parallel-eye autostereograms entirely in your browser. No Pyto or server required.

1. Upload your image

A photograph does not contain real depth information. For convincing faces and cubes, upload a proper grayscale depth map. Use the built-in cube for calibration.

2. Configure the stereogram

For 43 × 80 inches at 150 DPI, output would be 6450 × 12000 pixels, which may exceed mobile browser memory. Test at 1200 × 1600 first. Print scaling changes the physical repeat size.

Ready. Upload a depth map or select Calibration cube.

3. Preview

Parallel-eye viewing: relax your focus as though looking through the image. Two guide dots near the top are spaced one pattern period apart.

Depth map preview

Offline-capable static HTML/JavaScript. This tool uses row-wise depth-displaced repeating patterns, not full binocular ray tracing or complete occlusion correction. It is an experimental stereogram generator; perceptual fusion is not guaranteed.
